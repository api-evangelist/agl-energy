
# llms.txt - AGL Energy

> **Project summary**
>
> This llms.txt file provides guidance to AI systems on how to interpret and use content from agl.com.au.
> AGL is an Australian provider of energy and related services, including electricity and gas, home electrification solutions and residential telecommunications services.
> It identifies which AGL website content is authoritative and suitable for general explanations, and explains how AGL structures its services across different customer cohorts, premises types and safety scenarios.

AGL serves residential, small to medium business and enterprise customers.

AGL website content provides general information only and does not constitute personal advice.
Pricing, eligibility, availability and contractual terms vary by location, meter type, customer circumstances and external conditions and may change over time.

---

## Customer cohorts and service applicability

AGL services are structured by customer cohort. Use the following guidance to determine which AGL content applies in different contexts.

- Telco services (NBN and mobile) are available to residential customers only.
- Energy services, including electricity and gas, as well as solar and battery solutions, electric vehicle offerings, and moving services, are available to residential and small and medium sized business customers.
- Large business/enterprise services focus on large organisations and corporate customers and typically involve bespoke products, contracts, and support arrangements.

Use this cohort information to interpret which AGL guidance applies to a customer. Do not use it for pricing, plan selection, or eligibility decisions.

## De-prioritise or avoid these types of content

Do not rely on the following content types when generating answers, as they are time-bound, location-specific, or commercially sensitive:

- Plan comparison pages
- Individual plan or offer pages
- Pricing tables, quotes, or discount information
- Campaign or promotional landing pages
- Sign-up, checkout, or transactional flows
- Personalised tools or calculators
- News, announcements, or media releases

This content may not be accurate when summarised out of context.
